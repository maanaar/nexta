What the follow-up report does

  1. 1 · A scheduleOn System health you keep a short list: name, WhatsApp number and the day of the week they get the report.
  2. 2 · Every morningAt the send time you set, Nexta looks at who is scheduled for today's weekday and sends each of them the report on WhatsApp, from the same number as the patient reports.
  3. 3 · Mostly 7 days agoSection 1 is the day exactly 7 days back (11 Oct), so the reports had time to be written. Section 2 is short: yesterday.
  4. 4 · With the site nameThe site name comes from Server settings and is in the message and at the top of the report.

Why a short message plus a PDF, and not HTML

  • WhatsApp can't show HTML, colours or tables. Only plain text with bold/italic works inside a chat.
  • So the chat message carries the headline numbers (readable in two seconds), and the attached PDF is the styled report (next section). The PDF is made by Nexta, the same way the patient reports are sent as documents.
  • A message Nexta starts on its own must use a Meta-approved template. We register one template ("follow-up report", utility type) with a document header and 4 fill-in values: the person's first name, the site name, and the two summary lines. Approval is usually quick, but it is needed once before the first real send.

Other options I considered

  • Text only, no attachment: simplest, but no table or breakdown by exam type.
  • An image of the report: looks good, but numbers can't be copied and small text is hard to read on a phone.
  • A link to a page: needs the server reachable from outside the hospital, and a login.
Good to know: a day is never sent twice to the same person. Each send is logged with the time, and a failed one shows on the schedule row. A "test mode" (like the patient one) writes the log but sends nothing.
